Flash your AI Thinker ESP32-CAM directly from your browser.

AI Thinker ESP32-CAM

📷 Camera: OV2640
📐 Resolution: 240 × 240
⚡ Chip: ESP32

How to Flash

1. Connect USB-TTL to ESP32-CAM.
2. Connect GPIO 0 to GND.
3. Click the Install button above.
4. Select your ESP32 COM port.
5. Flash the firmware.
6. Disconnect GPIO 0 from GND and reset.
⚠️ Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Web Serial is required.
