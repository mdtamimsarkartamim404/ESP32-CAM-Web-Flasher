ESP32-CAM Web Flasher

Flash your AI Thinker ESP32-CAM directly from your browser. No Arduino IDE required during flashing.

🔧 Device Information

Board AI Thinker ESP32-CAM
Camera OV2640
Resolution 240 × 240
Firmware ESP32-CAM Control Panel
⚡ Connect & Flash ESP32-CAM ❌ Your browser does not support Web Serial. ❌ Web Serial is not allowed.

📋 How to Flash

1

Connect your ESP32-CAM to your computer using a USB-to-TTL programmer.

2

Put the ESP32-CAM into programming mode by connecting GPIO 0 to GND.

3

Connect the USB programmer and click Connect & Flash ESP32-CAM.

4

Select the serial port and allow the browser to flash the firmware.

5

Wait until flashing reaches 100%. Then disconnect GPIO 0 from GND and reset the ESP32-CAM.

⚠️ Important: Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a desktop computer. Web Serial generally does not work in Firefox. Make sure the correct ESP32 firmware binary is hosted in the firmware folder.
